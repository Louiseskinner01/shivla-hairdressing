About

Hair is more than
a style. It's identity.

Hi, I'm Shivla — a London-based hairdresser with a passion for making every client feel like the best version of themselves. My approach is personal, precise and always tailored to you.

Whether you're after a bold transformation or a refined trim, I take the time to understand your hair, your lifestyle and your vision before a single pair of scissors comes out.

Based in London and available for in-salon and mobile appointments. Let's create something beautiful together.
