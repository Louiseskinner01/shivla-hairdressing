About

Hair is more than
a style. It's identity.

I've been hairdressing since the age of 15, building a career across some of London's most prestigious salons and hotels and working with wonderful clients along the way.

Whether you're after a bold transformation or a refined trim, I take the time to understand your hair, your lifestyle and your vision before a single pair of scissors comes out.

With decades of experience and a genuine passion for what I do, I believe great hair is about making you feel confident, comfortable and completely yourself.

Previous clients have included Joanna Lumley, Poppy Delevingne, Twiggy and Sadie Frost.
